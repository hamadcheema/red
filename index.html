A CLEAR WORKFLOW

From first brief
to playable build.

Each project starts with a discussion about its goals, platform, available assets and technical requirements. The scope is then agreed before development begins. Depending on the project, work may include gameplay implementation, integrating supplied assets, interface implementation, testing and preparing a build for review.

Visual design, original 3D production, publishing and other specialist work are included only when specifically agreed. No project results, delivery dates or platform compatibility are guaranteed before the requirements are reviewed.
