Privacy policy.

A simple explanation of information shared when you contact us.

LAST UPDATED: 7 NOVEMBER 2026

Information you provide

When you email The Red Sparrow, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address, name and message. We use it to read, manage and respond to your enquiry. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your enquiry.

Website data

This is a static website hosted through GitHub Pages and delivered with Cloudflare services. These providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser and device details, request data and security logs to deliver, maintain and protect the site.

Email services

Our business email is handled using Cloudflare Email Routing, Google Gmail and Brevo SMTP services. These providers may process the contents and technical metadata of messages to route, store, deliver and protect email communications. Their handling of information is also subject to their own privacy terms.

Cookies and analytics

Cloudflare security features may use necessary cookies or similar technologies to help protect the website. We do not currently use website analytics or advertising trackers on this site.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell the personal information you send to us. We may share it with the service providers described above where needed to operate the website or handle your enquiry. Correspondence may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to respond and manage related follow-up, and then deleted when no longer required, subject to applicable requirements.

Third-party services

Links or services provided by third parties are governed by their own privacy policies. For information about GitHub Pages, please review GitHub’s privacy statement.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, email admin@theredsparrow.online.

This page is a general website privacy notice, not legal advice. Review it for your actual business practices and applicable local requirements before relying on it as a formal legal policy.
