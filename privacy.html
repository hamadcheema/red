Privacy policy.

A simple explanation of information shared when you contact us.

LAST UPDATED: 2 NOVEMBER 2026

Information you provide

When you email The Red Sparrow, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address, name and message. We use it to read and respond to your enquiry.

Website data

This website is a static website hosted through GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information, such as server logs, to deliver and protect the website. We do not currently operate an account system, contact form or advertising tracker on this site.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell the personal information you send to us. Messages may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to handle your enquiry and related follow-up, subject to applicable requirements.

Third-party services

Links or services provided by third parties are governed by their own privacy policies. For information about GitHub Pages, please review GitHub’s privacy statement.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, email admin@theredsparrow.online.
