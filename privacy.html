Privacy policy.

A clear explanation of information handled when you visit our website or contact us.

LAST UPDATED: 17 NOVEMBER 2026

Who operates this website

This website is operated under the The Red Sparrow brand, an independent game-development brand. Use of the brand name does not, by itself, identify or claim a separately incorporated legal entity. If the legal operator changes following incorporation, this notice will be updated with the applicable legal details. For questions about the website or its handling of information, contact admin@theredsparrow.online.

Information you provide

When you use the contact form or email The Red Sparrow, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, subject and message. We use it only to review, manage and respond to your enquiry. Contact-form messages are stored in a private Cloudflare KV workspace for up to 90 days and then expire automatically. The form applies a basic daily submission limit to reduce spam. Please do not submit sensitive personal information.

Website hosting and technical data

This is a static website hosted through GitHub Pages and delivered with Cloudflare services. These providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser and device details, request data and security logs to deliver, maintain and protect the site. Cloudflare security features may use necessary cookies or similar technologies for these purposes.

Email services

Messages sent to admin@theredsparrow.online, hammad@theredsparrow.online, accounts@theredsparrow.online and support@theredsparrow.online are currently forwarded by Cloudflare Email Routing to our Gmail inbox. The Red Sparrow may use Brevo as an outbound email service; when we send messages through Brevo, it may process message content, recipient details and delivery metadata. The website provides a contact form and direct email links. It does not currently offer a mailing-list signup. Cloudflare, Google and any email service providers used process information under their own service terms and privacy notices.

Advertising and Google AdSense

When advertising is enabled on this website, we may display ads served by Google AdSense. Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies and similar technologies, including web beacons, and may process information such as IP addresses or browser and device identifiers as a result of ad serving.

Google and its partners may use advertising cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's previous visits to this website or other websites. Depending on advertising settings and applicable consent choices, visitors may see personalized or non-personalized ads. Non-personalized ads may still use cookies or similar technologies for purposes such as frequency capping and aggregated ad reporting.

Visitors can manage Google's personalized advertising settings at Google Ads Settings. Visitors can also learn about opting out of some third-party vendors' personalized advertising at YourAdChoices. Google explains how it uses information on partner sites at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Information about Google's ad technology partners is available in its AdSense ad technology partners guidance.

Third-party advertising vendors and ad networks may place or read cookies in visitors' browsers, or use web beacons and IP addresses, to provide and measure ads. Those providers' data practices are governed by their own privacy notices. More information about Google's privacy practices is available in the Google Privacy Policy.

Cookies and analytics

Cookies are small files stored by a website or service in a browser. In addition to the advertising and security technologies described above, we do not currently use a separate third-party website analytics service. You can manage or block cookies through your browser settings, although some website or advertising features may not work as intended.

Consent and regional choices

Advertising technologies and cookies are subject to applicable legal requirements and the consent choices available to visitors. The availability and type of advertising may depend on those choices and the visitor's location. This privacy notice is not itself a consent-management interface. Where required, the relevant privacy messages and advertising controls must be configured and published in Google AdSense Privacy & messaging or through another suitable consent-management platform. The presence of this policy page alone does not establish that a consent message is active or that consent has been recorded.

Your privacy requests

You can contact us to ask about personal information you have sent to us, request that inaccurate information be corrected, ask for information to be deleted, or raise a privacy concern. Email admin@theredsparrow.online and describe your request. We may ask for reasonable information to verify that the request relates to you. We will consider and respond to requests in accordance with applicable requirements; some information may need to be retained where required or where it is still reasonably needed for a legitimate operational purpose.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell the personal information you send to us. We may share it with the service providers described in this policy where needed to operate the website, deliver advertising or handle your enquiry. Correspondence may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to respond and manage related follow-up, and then deleted when no longer required, subject to applicable requirements.

Third-party services and links

Links and services provided by third parties are governed by their own privacy policies. We do not control the privacy practices of those providers. You can review the relevant provider's policy before using its service.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, email admin@theredsparrow.online.

This page is a general website privacy notice, not legal advice. It should be kept accurate as the website's services, advertising settings and data practices change, and reviewed against applicable legal requirements.
