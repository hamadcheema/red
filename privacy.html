Privacy policy.

A clear explanation of information handled when you visit our website or contact us.

LAST UPDATED: 20 NOVEMBER 2026

Who operates this website

This website is operated under The Red Sparrow brand, an independent game-development brand. Use of the brand name does not, by itself, identify or claim a separately incorporated legal entity. If the legal operator changes following incorporation, this notice will be updated with the applicable legal details. For questions about the website or its handling of information, contact admin@theredsparrow.online.

Information you provide

When you use the contact form or email The Red Sparrow, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, subject and message. We use it only to review, manage and respond to your enquiry. Contact-form messages are stored in a private Cloudflare KV workspace for up to 90 days and then expire automatically. To enforce the daily submission limit, the form stores a keyed, one-way HMAC identifier derived from the request IP address; this identifier expires within 24 hours, and the raw IP address is not stored in the enquiry record. Cloudflare may separately process IP addresses as part of delivering and protecting the site. Please do not submit sensitive personal information.

Website hosting and technical data

This is a static website hosted through GitHub Pages and delivered with Cloudflare services. These providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser and device details, request data and security logs to deliver, maintain and protect the site. Cloudflare security features may use necessary cookies or similar technologies for these purposes.

Admin workspace and stored content

The private admin workspace uses GitHub sign-in and a short-lived, secure, HttpOnly session cookie. Temporary sign-in state expires after 10 minutes, and admin sessions expire after up to 8 hours. The workspace stores unpublished page and content drafts in Cloudflare KV for up to 90 days. Admin activity records, which may include the authorized account name and the action performed, are retained for up to 180 days for security and troubleshooting. Images deliberately uploaded for website content are stored in the website's GitHub repository until removed; deleted media copies are kept for up to 7 days to allow recovery. These systems are used to operate and maintain the website, not to create visitor advertising profiles.

Email services

Messages sent to admin@theredsparrow.online, hammad@theredsparrow.online, accounts@theredsparrow.online and support@theredsparrow.online are currently forwarded by Cloudflare Email Routing to our Gmail inbox. The Red Sparrow may use Brevo as an outbound email service; when we send messages through Brevo, it may process message content, recipient details and delivery metadata. The website provides a contact form and direct email links. It does not currently offer a mailing-list signup. Cloudflare, Google and any email service providers used process information under their own service terms and privacy notices.

Cookies and analytics

Cookies are small files stored by a website or service in a browser. We do not currently use a separate third-party website analytics service. Cloudflare security features may use necessary cookies or similar technologies to protect the site. You can manage or block cookies through your browser settings, although some website features may not work as intended.

Your privacy requests

You can contact us to ask about personal information you have sent to us, request that inaccurate information be corrected, ask for information to be deleted, or raise a privacy concern. Email admin@theredsparrow.online and describe your request. We may ask for reasonable information to verify that the request relates to you. We will consider and respond to requests in accordance with applicable requirements; some information may need to be retained where required or where it is still reasonably needed for a legitimate operational purpose.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell the personal information you send to us. We may share it with the service providers described in this policy where needed to operate the website or handle your enquiry. Correspondence may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to respond and manage related follow-up, and then deleted when no longer required, subject to applicable requirements.

Third-party services and links

Links and services provided by third parties are governed by their own privacy policies. We do not control the privacy practices of those providers. You can review the relevant provider's policy before using its service.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, email admin@theredsparrow.online.

This page is a general website privacy notice, not legal advice. It should be kept accurate as the website's services and data practices change, and reviewed against applicable legal requirements.
