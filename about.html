A LITTLE ABOUT US

Built around

The Red Sparrow is an independent creative brand focused on developing games and interactive experiences with Unity.

OUR APPROACH

Creative thinking.
Careful execution.

We believe a good game brings together a clear idea, satisfying interaction and a world players want to explore. Our work centres on Unity development, supported by 3D asset integration, game interface design and mobile-focused experiences.

We aim to keep collaboration straightforward: understand the goal, agree on a realistic scope and build toward a playable result. The Red Sparrow is presented as an independent game-development brand; project delivery is scoped individually rather than implying a large in-house team.

How a project
takes shape.

01 — Understand the brief. Discuss the gameplay idea, target platform, requirements and available assets.

02 — Agree on scope. Confirm the features, responsibilities, deliverables and practical constraints before work starts.

03 — Build and integrate. Implement the agreed Unity systems and integrate client-supplied or properly licensed assets.

04 — Review and hand off. Test the agreed build, address in-scope issues and provide the agreed deliverables.

Visual design, original art or 3D production, publishing and other specialist services are not assumed to be included; they must be agreed separately and may require client-supplied assets or external specialists.
